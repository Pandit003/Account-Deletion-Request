Data Deletion & Retention Policy

Google Play Data Safety Compliant

Upon processing your request, the following data associated with your account will be permanently removed:

  • Account Details: Profile information, email address, display name, profile picture, FCM tokens, and authentication records.
  • Created Questions & Exams: All question banks, tests, and answer keys hosted or created under your account.
  • Saved Questions: Bookmarked, saved, and flagged practice questions.
  • Test History & Analytics: Exam attempt logs, test results, performance analytics, and leaderboard statistics.
  • Notifications & Activity: System notifications, user alerts, and message history.

Processing Timeframe: Account deletion requests are scheduled immediately and processed permanently within 7 days. Once deleted, data cannot be recovered.
